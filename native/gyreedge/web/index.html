The floating markers are SlugHorn (Slug, Lengyel 2017): their outlines come out of SlugHorn's real C++ core compiled to WebAssembly as a curve/band texture pair, and are filled per-pixel by the real ported Slug fragment shader, depth-tested against this three.js room in one shared WebGL2 context.

Honest scope: SlugHorn's WASM pipeline is live here, but real glyph text is not wired yet -- these are hand-authored star outlines, not font glyphs. No FreeType binding exists upstream or here.
